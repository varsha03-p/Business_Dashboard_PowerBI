The Dashboard gains the business knowledge
📊 Business Dashboard — Power BI

📌 Project Overview

This project is a Business Dashboard created using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, product categories, regional sales, unit costs, discounts, and customer activity.

The dashboard presents business data through interactive charts and visualizations to help users understand and compare business performance.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze quantity sold by product category.
- Compare unit costs by customer type and sales representative.
- Compare sales amount and unit cost across product categories.
- Analyze sales amount by region.
- Study quantity sold and discounts across regions.
- Compare sales-date counts by product category and sales representative.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Business Data Analysis
- Dashboard Design

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

1. KPI Card: Displays the total unit cost.
2. Bar Chart: Shows quantity sold by product category.
3. Clustered Bar Chart: Compares unit cost by customer type and sales representative.
4. Line Chart: Compares sales amount and unit cost by product category.
5. Column Chart: Displays sales amount by region.
6. Area and Column Chart: Shows quantity sold by product category.
7. Area Chart: Compares quantity sold and discounts by region.
8. Stacked Area Chart: Displays sales-date counts by product category and sales representative.

🔍 Key Analysis Questions

- Which product category has the highest quantity sold?
- How do unit costs differ between new and returning customers?
- Which region generates the highest sales amount?
- How do discounts vary across regions?
- How does sales activity differ among product categories and sales representatives?

💡 Project Benefits

- Provides a clear overview of business performance.
- Makes it easier to compare sales across categories and regions.
- Helps identify trends and patterns in business data.
- Presents complex data in an understandable visual format.
- Supports data-driven business decisions.
